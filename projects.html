PROJECT 01 // CASE COMPETITION PROPOSAL

MITTI Compass: Financial & Expansion Simulator

CONTEXT: NATIONAL CASE CHALLENGE • BANGALORE FINAL ROUND (NOV 2026)

SELECTED OFFLINE FINALIST

The Challenge & Concept

MITTI Cáfe is an inspiring award-winning social enterprise operating cáfes staffed by persons with physical, intellectual, and psychiatric disabilities. The core strategic challenge was evaluating sustainable geographical expansion without diluting unit margins or mission integrity.

As a competition proposal for the MITTI Cáfe Case Challenge, our team designed MITTI Compass: an interactive financial and expansion-planning simulator created to help leadership stress-test location parameters, operating models, capital requirements, and return horizons.

Important Editorial Clarification: MITTI Compass was developed as a competition concept for the case challenge and selected for the offline final round in Bangalore in November 2026. It is presented here strictly as an analytical proposal and educational demonstration, not an officially deployed corporate infrastructure.
SIMULATOR ARCHITECTURE
  • OPERATING ARCHETYPE

    Lighthouse Hubs vs. Satellite Spokes

  • FINANCIAL ENGINE

    Sensitivity Modeling • CapEx & Unit Breakeven

  • GEOGRAPHIC LOGIC

    Cluster Density & Supply Chain Proximity

  • MY CONTRIBUTION

    Marketing & Financial Thinking • Positioning • Feasibility

CORE SYSTEM DIMENSIONS
01 / Spokes vs. Lighthouses

Distinguished between flagship 'Lighthouses' (high-visibility cultural & community hubs driving brand awareness) and lean 'Spokes' (compact kiosk formats in corporate IT parks delivering high-frequency, repeatable revenue).

02 / Geographical Expansion

Evaluated tier-1 vs. tier-2 city expansion paths based on footfall density, institutional corporate partnership potential, municipal ease of operation, and local supply chain costs.

03 / Financial & Funding Mechanics

Balanced CSR grant capital with commercial debt and internally generated operating cash flows. Modeled payback periods across varying rental agreements and fixed CapEx outlays.

04 / Customer Acquisition & Brand

Formulated value proposition positioning that anchors quality of culinary offerings and community connection, avoiding transactional charity messaging in favor of authentic consumer loyalty.

DIAGRAM: MITTI COMPASS OPERATING ARCHITECTURE SYSTEM SCHEMATIC

My Key Responsibilities & Insights:

  • • Marketing & Financial Thinking: Modeled break-even occupancy rates and customer lifetime value (LTV) against corporate site acquisition costs.
  • • Customer Acquisition Strategy: Formulated structured B2B corporate partnerships (cafeteria integrations) to provide reliable recurring cash flows.
  • • Brand Positioning: Framed the core messaging around product excellence and inclusion without patronizing charity tropes.
  • • Expansion Strategy & Finalist Selection: Defended the simulator logic leading to selection for the prestigious offline final round in Bangalore in November 2026.
PROJECT 02 // PRACTICAL ENTREPRENEURSHIP

BrightPath Home Tuition

AMRITSAR, PUNJAB • FOUNDER & INSTRUCTOR • COMMERCE

MICRO-VENTURE

Context & Execution

BrightPath Home Tuition was a small-scale, self-initiated entrepreneurial venture delivering customized home tutoring to commerce students. Rather than viewing tutoring as casual pocket money, I approached it as a live study in service delivery, unit pricing, customer trust, and operational scheduling.

I took full responsibility for teaching and student interaction, diagnosing learning bottlenecks, structuring weekly milestone reviews, managing student relationships, and coordinating transparently with parents regarding academic progress.

During the operational period, the venture generated approximately ₹8,500 in reported revenue, achieving complete cash self-sufficiency with zero external capital.

VENTURE METRICS & PROFILE
  • REPORTED REVENUE

    ≈ ₹8,500

  • DOMAIN TAUGHT

    Commerce • Accountancy • Business Studies

  • PRICING DISCIPLINE

    Per-Hour Value Benchmark vs. Monthly Retention

  • RETENTION RATE

    100% Student Continuity Over The Reported Term

PRACTICAL BUSINESS LESSONS
Service Delivery & Pedagogy

Discovered that pedagogical clarity is identical to product UX: concepts must be modularized into bite-sized milestones with immediate diagnostic feedback.

Pricing Psychology & Value

Tested hourly rates against monthly retainers. Parents strongly preferred bundled monthly commitments paired with structured bi-weekly progress reports.

Customer Acquisition via Trust

Zero advertising spend. All client conversions occurred through localized word-of-mouth recommendations driven by academic performance improvements.
