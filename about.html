01 / INTELLECTUAL FOUNDATION

Curiosity Grounded in Business Reality

I have always been fascinated by how economic value is created, distributed, and sustained. Long before analyzing balance sheets, I was drawn to the underlying mechanics of transactions: why people choose one service over another, how pricing reflects trust, and where margin leaks occur.

My academic journey blends B.Com (Honours) in Data Analytics at Guru Nanak Dev University with the BBA in Digital Business & Entrepreneurship at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore (IIMB). This dual academic vantage point shapes my core worldview: modern business requires both classical financial discipline and contemporary analytical literacy.

To me, data analytics is not merely about writing queries or generating visualizations; it is an investigative tool to question assumptions. A spreadsheet or dashboard is valuable only if it leads to sharper business choices, clearer risk allocation, and measurable operational improvement.

“The true test of analytical skill is not computational complexity, but whether it clarifies the strategic path forward.”

— Personal Notebook, 2026

Whether participating in national-level case competitions (such as IIT Delhi or MITTI Cáfe's national finals) or running a bootstrapped micro-venture like BrightPath Home Tuition, I treat every project as an empirical laboratory. I prioritize asking the right questions before jumping into models.
